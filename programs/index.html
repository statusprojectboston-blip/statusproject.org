Programs

What we do

Pathways to stability: housing, health and recovery, community support, and opportunities and empowerment.

Status Project is building a community-rooted way of working: low-barrier navigation, benefits and health-system support, warm referrals, persistent follow-up, and multilingual community engagement. The aim is to help residents move from crisis toward health, housing stability, safety, economic mobility, and reintegration.

We improve access. We do not provide clinical diagnosis, treatment, or medical services. Clinical, legal, and other regulated services belong with qualified professionals and partner organizations. Contact us to confirm what support is available right now. Verified program outcomes will be shared as they become available.

Pathway

Housing stability

Improving access to housing stability, including rent assistance and connections to housing, benefits, and community resources.

Pathway

Health & recovery

Improving access to behavioral health and substance-use recovery resources, health navigation, and accompaniment to hospital visits.

Pathway

Community support

Inclusive, culturally responsive community support, in a person’s own language and with respect for their culture.

Pathway

Opportunities & empowerment

Opportunities for lasting stability and dignity, including translation and help finding a path through asylum and work-permit applications.
