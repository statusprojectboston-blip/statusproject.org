Status Project earns Candid’s 2026 Gold Seal of Transparency

On October 22, 2026, Status Project earned Candid’s 2026 Gold Seal of Transparency. Status Project shares its goals, strategies, and capabilities publicly on its Candid nonprofit profile (opens in a new tab).

The organization

Real people. Stronger futures.

Status Project helps Boston individuals and families find housing, health, recovery, community support, and opportunity, in their own language.

Need guidance? Write to statusprojectboston@gmail.com or call (508) 425-1299. Want to help? Give to the Boston Community Impact Fund, or volunteer with us.

More access. More opportunity. A more inclusive Boston.

Our mission is to improve access to housing stability, behavioral health and substance-use recovery resources, community support, health and social-service navigation, and opportunities for lasting stability and dignity.

A first step, in your language.

Rent assistance. Asylum and work-permit applications. Translation. Hospital accompaniment. Housing, benefits, health, and community resources.

Not sure where to start? Send a message and we will help you find the right path.

Pathways to stability.

Housing stability. Health and recovery. Community support. Opportunities and empowerment. This is how we work toward a stronger Boston for all.

An open door. A continuous path.

Our values: access, connection, stability, inclusion, and progress.

The fund

The Boston Community Impact Fund is open.

It is an initiative to strengthen pathways to housing stability, essential resources, and community support for individuals and families across Greater Boston. Your support helps provide housing, health, recovery, opportunities, and pathways for more inclusive communities.
