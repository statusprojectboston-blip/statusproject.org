Immigrant and Multilingual Navigation

Coordinated navigation, referrals and community stabilization under the STATUS PROJECT 2026-2031 canonical plan. Program availability depends on funding, staffing and qualified partners.

What this service area covers

Inclusive services supports people facing language, immigration, cultural, and access barriers. Navigation includes multilingual assistance, connections to qualified immigration legal providers, housing and benefits resources, culturally responsive referrals, and access to health and community services.

How to connect

  1. Contact STATUS PROJECT with a general service inquiry.
  2. Discuss needs and available navigation options.
  3. Receive referral information for suitable qualified partners.
  4. Arrange follow-up when available.

Availability: STATUS PROJECT is developing services in phases. Referrals and direct assistance depend on active partnerships, funding, staffing, and authorization. Do not submit confidential case information by ordinary email.
