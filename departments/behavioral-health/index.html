Behavioral Health & Mental Health Access

Program scope

Nonclinical navigation; culturally responsive referral to licensed mental-health providers; care coordination; peer and community support; crisis referral pathways.

Who this program serves

Residents of Greater Boston experiencing overlapping barriers to stability, including housing insecurity, poverty, health and recovery needs, language barriers, justice-system involvement and social exclusion. Eligibility and referrals depend on individual circumstances and available partner resources.

Service pathway

Community outreach or referral; preliminary navigation and needs assessment; consent and privacy review where applicable; individualized stabilization and referral planning; warm handoffs to qualified partner agencies; and follow-up on referral outcomes.

Implementation and partnerships

This program is part of the approved institutional planning portfolio. STATUS PROJECT is pursuing phased implementation, qualified provider partnerships, staffing, funding and appropriate facilities. Clinical, legal, reimbursable or regulated services will be provided only by authorized professionals or partner organizations until the institution satisfies applicable requirements.

Program availability: Contact STATUS PROJECT to verify current intake, service capacity, partner referral options and eligibility. This page describes the institutional program model and does not claim that all services are currently operating.
