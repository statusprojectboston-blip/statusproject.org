Edson G. Suarez Silva

Clerk (Corporate Secretary) & Co-Founder

Edson G. Suarez Silva is Clerk (Corporate Secretary) and a co-founder of Status Project Inc. He is a bilingual (English and Spanish) events specialist, with more than seven years in hospitality and logistics.

Since 2017 he has been Lead Front-of-House Associate at Gourmet Catering in Natick. Earlier he was a storage and merchandise supervisor in Venezuela.

He holds an associate degree in Foreign Trade from Caracas.
