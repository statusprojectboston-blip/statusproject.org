Cristian Sleither Castillo Garnica

Director of Community Affairs & External Relations

Cristian Sleither Castillo Garnica is Director of Community Affairs and External Relations of Status Project Inc. He is a native Spanish speaker with a background in construction and facilities maintenance, including water and sewer infrastructure and hotel maintenance.

He leads community affairs and external relations for Status Project. He builds partnerships with Boston organizations and community leaders, and he connects Status Project with Boston neighbors.
