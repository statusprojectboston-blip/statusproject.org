Elizabeth Castro

Treasurer

Elizabeth Castro is Treasurer of Status Project Inc.

She handles accounts payable at Humanitary Medical Center Inc., using Oracle NetSuite and Bill.com. Since 2022 she first worked there as a social worker helping seniors with benefits and housing applications.

From 1999 to 2013 she was Assistant Director and Case Manager of the Parent Aide Program at Centro Las Americas in Worcester, and she coordinated a health-care-law outreach campaign.

She holds a Bachelor’s in Business Administration from the Catholic University of Puerto Rico.
