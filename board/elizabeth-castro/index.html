Elizabeth Castro

Treasurer

Elizabeth Castro is Treasurer of Status Project Inc. and a member of the 2026 Board. She is a co-founder of the organization.

She brings financial leadership to Status Project, with a commitment to handling every gift with integrity, transparency, and care. She holds a deep belief that stability opens doors to opportunity.

Her stewardship is aimed at the purpose of the organization: helping Boston families find housing stability, resources, and a path forward.
