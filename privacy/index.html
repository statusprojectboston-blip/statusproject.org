Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

This policy describes how the Status Project website treats information. It is not a substitute for advice from your own lawyer. The Terms of Use cover copyright and the rules for using the site.

This website

Status Project Inc. does not put tracking scripts, advertising cookies, or analytics tools on this website. You do not create an account to read it. The pages do not ask for a payment card.

Email

If you write to statusprojectboston@gmail.com, Status Project uses that message to read and reply. Please do not send client case records, medical information, or other confidential records by ordinary email.

Embedded video

Status Project Voices can show an interviews playlist in a player from youtube-nocookie.com. Until a playlist is added, that player is not loaded. When it is shown, the player is served by Google, and Google’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab) governs what the player does. Podcast links, when they are added, leave this site for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, or YouTube Music.

Donations

Donations are not processed on this website. They are processed by Zeffy under Zeffy’s terms. The button opens Zeffy’s donation form.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to statusprojectboston@gmail.com.
