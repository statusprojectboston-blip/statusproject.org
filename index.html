Our Mission & Vision

STATUS PROJECT INC. is a Boston-based nonprofit dedicated to helping people facing intersecting barriers access practical support, trusted referrals and opportunities for long-term stability. Our inclusive community stabilization model is being developed through collaboration with qualified service providers, institutions and community partners.

Our vision is a Greater Boston where every person can find a pathway toward dignity, health, safe housing and economic participation.
