Our Purpose
01

People do not live in separate systems.

Housing, health, recovery, safety and economic stability are interconnected. Our work is designed around that reality.


STATUS PROJECT INC. is a Boston-based community organization working with people navigating multiple barriers at the same time. We create connections across services and opportunities so that a person can move from crisis toward greater stability.

Integrated Support

Multiple needs.
One connected pathway.

Our model brings together the areas that most directly affect stability, health and opportunity.

01 / HOUSING

Housing Stability

Navigation and support focused on housing access, housing stability, homelessness and connections to available housing resources.

02 / HEALTH

Health & Public Health

Connections to healthcare, HIV and hepatitis resources, prevention, education and community-based health support.

03 / RECOVERY

Behavioral Health & Recovery

Navigation and community support for people affected by mental health challenges and substance use disorders.

04 / COMMUNITY

Community Navigation

Help understanding systems, identifying resources, coordinating services and creating practical next steps.

05 / SAFETY

Safety & Reintegration

Connections for people navigating violence, reentry, displacement and other circumstances that create barriers to stability.

06 / OPPORTUNITY

Benefits & Economic Stability

Navigation toward benefits, community resources and opportunities that can strengthen long-term stability and independence.
