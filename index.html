Our mission / Nuestra misión

More access. More opportunity.
A more inclusive Boston.

STATUS PROJECT INC. advances community stabilization by building coordinated pathways to housing, health, safety, recovery, economic opportunity and trusted support. We are developing our institutional program portfolio in partnership with qualified organizations and providers.

STATUS PROJECT INC. promueve la estabilidad comunitaria mediante conexiones con vivienda, salud, seguridad, recuperación y oportunidades para todas las personas.
