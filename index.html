Our mission

STATUS PROJECT INC. is a Boston-based nonprofit focused on dignity, inclusion, and connecting underserved communities with resources and opportunities. Our institutional priorities address homelessness, economic instability, behavioral health, substance use, discrimination, and barriers to essential services.

Our program priorities

Housing & Stability

Housing navigation, homelessness response, and basic needs.

Health & Recovery

Behavioral health, recovery, harm reduction, and public-health referral pathways.

Community Navigation

Benefits, case-management pathways, and service coordination.

Inclusion & Reintegration

Immigrant navigation, LGBTQ+ inclusion, reentry, and survivor support.

Economic Opportunity

Workforce development, education, and economic stability pathways.

Community Partnerships

Developing collaborative, accessible referral and support networks.

Programs are institutional priorities and may be in development. Regulated clinical services require appropriate approvals and qualified partners.
