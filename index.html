Our mission

People first. Stronger futures.

STATUS PROJECT INC. is a Boston-based nonprofit building accessible pathways for people affected by homelessness, behavioral health challenges, substance use, economic instability, discrimination, and barriers to essential services. We prioritize dignity, coordinated community navigation, and inclusive partnerships.

Our history and vision

Built on community commitment

Established in Massachusetts, STATUS PROJECT INC. grew from a vision of connecting people facing overlapping challenges to practical support. After a founder-led development period, we are strengthening governance, fundraising readiness, technology, and partnerships to implement community programs in phases.

Our long-term vision is a trusted Greater Boston community stabilization and health access center. Implementation depends on verified funding, qualified staffing, and required approvals.

Programs and pathways

Housing Stability

Health and Recovery

Behavioral health, substance-use recovery, harm reduction, and public-health referrals.

Benefits, coordinated case-management pathways, and referrals.

Immigrant support navigation, LGBTQ+ inclusion, reentry, and survivor safety.

Economic Opportunity

Education, employment pathways, and economic stability.

Partnership Development

Working toward accessible referral networks and institutional collaboration.

These are organizational priorities, not a claim that all services are operational. Clinical and regulated services require qualified partners and applicable approvals.
