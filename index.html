STATUS PROJECT INC. is a Boston-based community organization committed to helping people facing housing instability, health barriers and social inequities connect with resources and opportunities that strengthen their lives.

About STATUS PROJECT

Our work is centered on people and communities experiencing barriers to stability and opportunity. We seek to strengthen access to housing resources, health and wellness support, benefits navigation, community services and other resources that help individuals move toward greater stability and independence.

Our Areas of Focus

Housing Stability

Connecting individuals and families with housing resources, stabilization support and community-based assistance.

Health & Wellness

Supporting navigation of health, behavioral health and community wellness resources.

Benefits Navigation

Helping community members identify and navigate available public and community resources.

Community Support

Connecting people experiencing barriers with organizations, programs and services that can help address their needs.

Everyone Deserves a Path Forward

STATUS PROJECT INC. works from a commitment to dignity, inclusion and equitable access. Our community focus includes people experiencing homelessness or housing instability and individuals and families navigating complex health, economic and social challenges.
